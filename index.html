Route 1: Lucknow → Chennai → VIT 🔄

Lucknow se train le, Chennai pahuncho, phir local transport se Perambur aur wahan se Katpadi via train.

⚠️ Note: Yeh route thoda complex hai — 2 trains + local transport + ek extra connection. Time aur energy zyada lagega. Agar galti se ek train miss ho gayi toh problem ho sakti hai!

Complete Journey Breakdown

~45–50 hrs total
🏠
Lucknow (Ghar se station)
Auto/cab leke Lucknow NE ya Charbagh station pahuncho. 30–45 min pehle pahunch jaana.
🚕 Auto: ₹50–150
🚂
Train: LJN MAS Express (16094)
Lucknow NE → MGR Chennai Central. Departs 16:20, arrives ~07:00 (next day+1). 38h 40m ki journey.
🎫 ₹2,135 (3A)
🚇
Chennai → Perambur (Local)
Chennai Central se Perambur station sirf 3–4 km door hai. Chennai Suburban train ya auto leke jao.
🚇 Suburban: ₹5–10 | Auto: ₹80–120
🚆
Train: Perambur → Katpadi Jn
Perambur se Katpadi ke liye kai trains milti hain. Distance ~135 km, lagbhag 2–2.5 ghante. Unreserved ya passenger train le sakte ho.
🎫 ₹40–200 (General/Sleeper)
🛺
Katpadi Jn → VIT Vellore
Auto rickshaw ya cab leke VIT campus pahuncho. Distance ~7 km, 15–20 min.
🚕 Auto: ₹80–120 | Cab: ₹150–200
🎓
VIT Vellore Campus! 🎉
Hostel report karo, fresher life shuru karo!

💡 Perambur se Katpadi trains:

Chennai Division mein Perambur ek important station hai. Katpadi ke liye Express trains jaise Chennai–Bangalore corridor ya Chennai–Vellore passenger trains milti hain. IRCTC pe "PER → KPD" search karo.

⏰ Total Cost (Approx)

Train 1 (LJN→MAS): ₹2,135 + Local Chennai: ₹100 + Train 2 (PER→KPD): ₹150 + Katpadi→VIT: ₹100 = ~₹2,500 total

Katpadi Station ke baare mein Tips 📍

📌 Station Exit pe kya karna hai?

Station ke main exit se bahar aao. Bahut saare auto drivers "VIT? VIT?" puchenge. Normal fare ₹80–100 hai, agar zyada maange toh negotiate karo ya Ola book karo.

🍽️ Bhuk lagi hai?

Katpadi station ke bahar aur VIT campus ke andar bahut saare canteens aur mess available hain. Station ke paas ek-do decent restaurants bhi milenge.

💰 Cash rakho!

Autos cash hi lete hain mostly. ₹200–300 cash pocket mein rakhna station se campus tak ke liye sufficient hai.

🎓 VIT First Year Tips (Bonus!)

• Reporting mein late mat ho — usually strict hota hai.
• Vellore ka weather hot & humid hai — light cotton kapde pack karna.
• Hostel mein electric kettle & iron allowed nahi hota (check karo).
• VIT ka mess food decent hai, panic mat karo!
